# CoachNotes 0.2.23

## Start the Day with a Plan

- Start of Day in Mission Control suggests clients with due or overdue to-dos,
  high-priority items, active flags, or no recorded note for seven days. Reasons
  and Keep on My Radar context stay visible. Adjust the suggestion criteria and
  inactivity threshold in Suggestion Settings.
- Select anyone with new messages, then drag clients or use the arrow buttons
  to choose your order. Suggest Order is optional; your chosen order stays put.
  You do not need to know your next action before reviewing a client.
- A persistent Today strip preserves your place while you visit profiles, ASK,
  sources, or Mission Control. Next only navigates. Done for Today, Later Today,
  Tomorrow, and Skip are separate choices. Unfinished reviews carry forward.
- Add a client's Weekly Review focus to Today so it stays beside their profile.
  End of Day includes chosen clients and reconciles completed reviews while
  preserving unfinished drafts and explicit future deferrals.

## Feedback When It Fits Your Work

1. Does the suggested selection/order give you a useful starting point, and is
   rearranging it comfortable?
2. Are Next, Done for Today, and the deferral choices clear in real work?
3. Can you leave a review, do something else, and easily find your place again?

## Rollout and Boundaries

Desktop-only update; no new AI calls, server contract, or model changes. Version
0.2.22 continues working, so install when convenient. Use Check Updates, download
the new installer, and quit CoachNotes before replacing the app. Apple Silicon
and Intel builds remain unsigned/unnotarized.

The ordering uses local rules, not AI judgments. No recorded note is not proof
that a client has not been contacted; CoachNotes does not read Everfit messages.
Done for Today marks a client reviewed, not their underlying coach to-dos done.
A saved note remains unfinished until its dashboard update succeeds. All daily
worklist data stays on this Mac. No demo clients or private notes are bundled.
