# CoachNotes 0.2.25

- Later Today now confirms the action and shows deferred clients with a
  **Review deferred** button. Clients deferred until tomorrow stay separate.
- The Today banner disappears when today's list is finished. You can also
  close it early without losing progress, then resume through
  **Mission Control > Today > Resume Review**.
- Hiding the banner does not skip clients or change End of Day inclusion.

Includes the End of Day finishing touches from 0.2.24. Quit CoachNotes before
installing. Your vault and notes are preserved. Choose arm64 for Apple Silicon
or x64 for Intel. This is a desktop-only update; the existing server and older
desktop remain usable.
