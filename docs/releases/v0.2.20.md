# CoachNotes 0.2.20

## Archive Clients Without Losing Their History

- Choose `Archive Client` on a client profile when coaching ends or pauses.
- Archived clients disappear from active search, Mission Control, due-task alerts, and weekly reviews.
- Find them under `Filters > Archived` above the client list. Search by name or bio tag there as usual.
- Profiles, notes, Ask, editing, and undo history remain available. Adding notes does not silently restore a client.
- Choose `Restore Client` to return them to active work. Archiving/restoring does not change their last-updated date.
- Saved weekly reviews hide archived clients immediately. Their practice-wide summary and patterns need regeneration if membership changed; remaining client assessments stay readable.

## Review Checklist

1. Archive a client and confirm their profile and notes are still accessible.
2. Switch to `Filters > Active`: they should not appear in search or Mission Control alerts.
3. Open the weekly review: they should be absent. Generate a new review when convenient to refresh the overall summary.
4. Find the client using `Filters > Archived` and try Ask or inspect their sources.
5. Restore the client and confirm their history and active alerts return.

## Rollout

Desktop-only release. No proxy changes, AI prompt changes, or coordinated server cutover are needed. Liz can install it when convenient and continue using 0.2.19 until then. This release uses the existing local archive fields and preserves client data. Builds remain unsigned, so macOS may require Right-click and Open on first launch.
